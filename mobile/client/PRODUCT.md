# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Philippine karinderya (small local eatery) owners and operators who generate used cooking oil. They are typically busy, operate in low-bandwidth or offline-heavy settings, and value clear, direct incentives (store discount vouchers) for recycling their oil. Secondary users include collection drivers and program owners monitoring metrics.

## Product Purpose
OilTrace is an IoT-enabled logistics and tracking platform for used cooking oil recycling in the Philippines. It grades collected oil for SAF (Sustainable Aviation Fuel) or biofuel production, records data on the Sepolia Ethereum testnet for transparency, and rewards consumers with points redeemable at partner stores.

## Brand Personality
- Community-focused (local, reliable, trustworthy, warm)
- Eco-innovative (clean tech, modern, forward-looking)
- Seamless/Premium (glassmorphic aesthetic, frictionless, professional)

## Anti-references
- SaaS template clichés (overly tracked small all-caps kickers, massive hero metrics, identical boring card grids)
- Cluttered, low-contrast, or overly flat interfaces that make information hard to read under natural Philippine daylight.

## Design Principles
1. **Context-Aware Spacing**: Use clean spacing and grouped hierarchy to prevent the visual fragmentation of multiple stacked cards.
2. **Accessible Contrast**: Maintain high text contrast and clear touch targets for busy operators in varying ambient light conditions.
3. **Intentional Glassmorphism**: Use glassmorphic panels and subtle highlights selectively to denote hierarchy and interactive quality without cluttering the screen.
4. **Visual Actionability**: Distinguish clearly between static information and interactive elements (e.g. settings links) through explicit visual signifiers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratios of at least 4.5:1 for body copy and 3.0:1 for displays.
- High-visibility touch targets (minimum 44x44px).
- Accommodate light, dark, and dim mode viewing preferences.
